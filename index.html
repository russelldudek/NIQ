07 / Discovery

The questions I would want on the table early.

  1. Which collection method would leadership most like to make unnecessary over the next three years?
  2. Where do global standards currently collide with local retailer knowledge or market reality?
  3. Which source types are commercially valuable but disproportionately expensive or slow to acquire?
  4. What would Finance need to believe by month six to fund the next wave of transformation?
  5. Which decisions should remain local even if the platforms and quality standards become global?

The goal is not maximum automation. It is a globally governed, locally intelligent collection portfolio that earns the right to scale.
