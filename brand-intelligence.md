## Typography evidence and decision

NIQ Brand Guidelines V2.2 identifies Aktiv Grotesk as the primary fact/data typeface and Utopia Std as the more expressive human voice.

Those font files are proprietary and are not copied into this repository. The site uses `Helvetica Neue / Helvetica / Arial` as a documented system substitute for the precise grotesk role and `Georgia` as a documented system substitute for the expressive serif role. The substitutes are not represented as official NIQ fonts.

## Visual grammar

The official guidelines emphasize a primarily blue narrative with controlled accent colors, clear data presentation and confident typography. The candidate experience adds a role-specific depth metaphor: source-data planes move toward a trusted source core as the visitor scrolls. The 3D effect is candidate-original and intentionally avoids copying an NIQ product interface.

## Motion character

NIQ's current public direction is tech-forward and AI-native. The campaign expresses that through restrained depth navigation and one-shot state changes rather than perpetual decorative motion. Reduced-motion users receive a complete vertical narrative with no 3D transitions.
