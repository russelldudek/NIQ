# NIQ Candidate Vision — Development Audit

Campaign state: **building**

## Current design correction

Russell requested a one-look experience that is highly visual, context-first, memorable, tactically empathetic, adversarial in its operating logic, and strong enough to earn a direct conversation CTA. He also requested a subtle star-like background that reinforces the depth-travel sensation.

### Rejected execution

The prior site had strong depth navigation, magnetic scene settling, and optical focus, but later scenes still behaved like an executive dashboard: equal-weight cards, explanatory copy, and numerical-looking source bars. The visual system was interesting while the information architecture remained too conventional.

### Approved correction

Rebuild the site as a **Signal Corridor** with one dominant idea per scene:

1. The Full View starts at the source.
2. NIQ's current AI moment makes upstream acquisition more strategic.
3. The current operating model protects knowledge the transformation cannot afford to lose.
4. Rebalance the collection portfolio rather than simply replacing field work.
5. Russell's transferable advantage is the repeated operating pattern: scale, signal, field, control.
6. Learn fast enough to change the right things in 30 / 90 / 180 days.
7. Ask three high-information executive questions.
8. End with a no-oriented, working-session CTA: `Pressure-test the model`.

The star field is implemented as a candidate-original **signal field**, not a space theme. It is sparse and nearly static at rest, stretches radially with scroll velocity, and settles when the focal scene snaps into place.

### Tactical empathy and adversarial structure

The key public argument is:

**Protect the knowledge. Change the mechanism.**

The site explicitly recognizes that field-heavy collection can carry retailer relationships, exception knowledge, and local judgment, while also making clear that preserving the current operating model solely to protect those assets is not durable. The core opposing pairs are faster/trusted, global/locally intelligent, partner-enabled/controlled, and lower cost/covered.

### Comparison integrity correction

The prior source-mix bars used illustrative numeric widths that could visually imply unsupported precision. The new model removes source percentages entirely. Each market condition assigns qualitative operating roles — `PRIMARY`, `SUPPORTING`, `EXCEPTION`, `SUNSET` — and keeps four non-negotiable decision invariants visible: trust, coverage, local intelligence, economics.

### CTA correction

Top-level Email and LinkedIn asks were removed from the header. The site now delivers the full argument before asking for contact. The closing CTA uses a Voss-like no-oriented question: `Would it be a bad idea to put this model against one real NIQ market and see where it breaks?` The primary action is `Pressure-test the model`.
